River Run & Forest Picnic

A perfect blend of adrenaline and relaxation! Navigate through moderate Class III rapids for two hours, then pull over at our secret riverside spot for an amazing artisanal picnic in the woods, featuring local fresh food.

Twilight Rapids & Stargazing

Experience the river like never before. We start our journey in the late afternoon to catch the sunset on the water. After navigating the rapids, we set up a riverside campfire to roast marshmallows and stargaze with a professional guide.

Extreme Rapids & Rope Swing

For the true thrill-seekers! Conquer intense Class IV and V rapids that will get your heart racing. The trip concludes at a deep canyon pool where you can test your courage on our giant cliff-side rope swing.
